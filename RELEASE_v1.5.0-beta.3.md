# FrostSeek Aura Tracker v1.5.0-beta.3

Non-live full-baseline integration build.

This build replaces the earlier simplified v1.5 prototype with the complete
Manastorm Recruiter 0.6.8 workflow, hosted directly inside FrostSeek.

Included from the full baseline:

- Recruitment parsing, follow-up questions, automatic replies and public-channel scanner.
- Role capacities, Aura reservations, pending-invite accounting and applicant management.
- Complete raid roster editor, stepwise group optimizer and Ready Check state.
- Manastorm start/validation, level warnings, protected leave actions and persisted rebuild recovery.
- Full settings, minimap control, commands, group chat and twenty editable message templates.

FrostSeek additions layered on top:

- Automatic Aura of Experience detection and provider ownership inference.
- Manual Aura synchronization between the recruiter and FrostSeek.
- Combat-based Tank and Healer inference.
- Correct Bonus XP artwork, Aura overlay, alerts and five-second entry audit.
- FrostSeek-native hosting, sizing, colors and tab lifecycle.

Upstream attribution and GPL terms are recorded in `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`
and `LICENSE`.

This is a verification build. Test protected and asynchronous raid actions in
a real raid before promotion.
